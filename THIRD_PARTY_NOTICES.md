## React Email

The standalone MJML examples in `templates/` adapt the templates, themes, public sample data, and artwork references from [React Email](https://github.com/resend/react-email/tree/674d8ec2804b86be5d99156383fb1810179847bf/apps/demo/emails), pinned at commit `674d8ec2804b86be5d99156383fb1810179847bf`. The complete source inventory and per-example provenance are recorded in `templates/manifest.json`; only examples with a checked-in `.mjml` file appear in the demo. `src/example.css` contains canvas-specific adaptations. Images remain hosted by the React Email demo, and web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Brand names and artwork are included as demonstration material, without endorsement. Sample prices, dates, addresses, receipts, and policy text are illustrative and may be historical.
